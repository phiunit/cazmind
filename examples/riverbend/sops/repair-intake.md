# Repair Intake SOP

## Check in a repair

1. Greet the customer and put the bike in the work stand.
2. Do a two-minute inspection: brakes, drivetrain, tires, headset, wheels true.
3. Write a repair ticket with the customer's name, phone, bike description and the work requested.
4. Quote the labor from the service menu and get a yes before any work starts.
5. Tag the bike with the ticket number and park it on the intake rack.

## Turnaround times

Flat fixes and brake adjustments are same day. A standard tune-up is two business days. Anything that needs parts ordered is quoted at the time the parts arrive.

## Calling the customer

Call when the work is done, or before doing anything that would push the bill more than 20 dollars over the quote.
