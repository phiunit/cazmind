# Welcome to Riverbend Bike Co

Riverbend is a 12-person bike shop with a full repair bench. We sell commuter, gravel and kids' bikes, and we fix anything with two wheels.

## Your first week

Day one is shadowing on the sales floor. Days two and three you sit with the repair bench and learn the intake process. By the end of the week you should be able to check in a repair on your own.

## Shop hours

We open at 10:00 and close at 19:00, Tuesday through Sunday. Mondays are closed for deep cleaning and inventory.

## Who to ask

Questions about the floor go to the floor lead. Questions about repairs go to the bench lead. Anything about pay or scheduling goes to the owner.
