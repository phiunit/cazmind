# Salary Bands

## Sales floor

Floor associates are paid 19 to 23 dollars an hour. The floor lead band is 25 to 29 dollars an hour.

## Repair bench

Mechanics are paid 22 to 28 dollars an hour depending on certification. The bench lead band is 30 to 34 dollars an hour.

## Review cycle

Bands are reviewed every January. Raises take effect on the first pay period of February.
