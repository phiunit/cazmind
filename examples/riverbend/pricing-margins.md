# Pricing and Margins

## Bike margins

We target a 38 percent gross margin on new bikes. Floor staff may discount up to 5 percent without approval. Anything deeper needs the floor lead.

## Labor pricing

Labor is billed at 90 dollars an hour. A standard tune-up is priced at 85 dollars and takes about 50 minutes of bench time.

## Parts markup

Parts are marked up 45 percent over distributor cost. Tires are marked up 40 percent to stay competitive.
